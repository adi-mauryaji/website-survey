Our dentists

Dr. Karim Adel

General and children's dentistry

Looks after the whole family: check-ups, cleaning and fillings for adults, and treatment for children from their first tooth.

Before your child's first visit

  • Tell your child about the visit in simple words, and avoid scary ones like “needle” or “pull”.
  • Pick a time after a nap or a meal, when your child is rested.
  • Bring any earlier X-rays or dental reports.

Dr. Hana Mostafa

Orthodontics

Braces for children, teens and adults. At the first visit she examines the teeth and explains the treatment plan and how long it should take.

When to check your child for braces

A first orthodontic check around age seven is recommended, even if the teeth look straight. Catching problems early keeps treatment simpler.

Questions parents ask

How much is a check-up?

A check-up and cleaning is EGP 600. Afterwards the dentist tells you what you need and what it costs, before starting any treatment.

When should my child first visit?

When the first tooth appears, or before their first birthday. Early visits are short and help your child get used to the clinic.

Can I book several family members on the same day?

Yes. Put the number of adults and children in your booking message, and we'll try to give you back-to-back appointments.

Are you open on Fridays?

No. We're open Saturday to Thursday, 10 am to 9 pm. You can send a WhatsApp message any time, and we'll reply during opening hours.
