Stay steady on your feet.

We are a physiotherapy clinic in Leeds for older people who have had a fall, or who worry they might. We find out what is making you unsteady, then help you build the strength and balance to keep doing the things you enjoy.

It may be time to see us if you:

  • have had a fall in the last year
  • feel unsteady when you stand up or walk
  • hold on to furniture to get around at home
  • go out less because you are worried about falling

What happens when you ring

Everyone starts the same way, whether you come to us on your own or someone books for you.

  1. 1

    Ring us

    We ask a few questions about what has been happening and book a time that suits you.

  2. 2

    Come for your assessment

    An hour with a physiotherapist. We test your balance, strength and walking, and talk through other things that can make a fall more likely, such as medicines, eyesight and shoes.

  3. 3

    Agree your plan

    We explain what we found in plain terms and suggest what will help most: exercises to do at home, a place in a class, or physiotherapy visits at home.

What to bring to your assessment

  • Your glasses, if you wear them
  • The shoes you wear most days
  • Your walking stick or frame, if you use one
  • A list of the medicines you take

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in.

Services and prices

Your assessment comes first, so we can recommend what will help you most.

Falls-risk assessment

£55 for 60 minutes

A full hour at the clinic with a physiotherapist. We look at balance, strength and walking, and at anything else that may be adding to your risk of a fall. You leave with a plan you understand.

Strength and balance classes

£12 a session

Groups of six, led by a physiotherapist, so there is time to help everyone. You can hold on to a chair for any exercise, and sit down whenever you need to. Ring us for class times.

Home-visit physiotherapy

£75 a visit

For people who find it hard to get to us. We come to homes within 5 miles of the clinic, and while we are there we can look at stairs, rugs and lighting with you.

Looking into this for a parent?

Many of our patients first hear about us from a son or daughter. Here is what helps to know.

  • You can ring and book for them. We will want a quick word with them too, so they know what to expect.
  • You are welcome to come to the assessment. We explain what we find to you both.
  • A home visit may suit them better if getting out is hard. We visit homes within 5 miles of the clinic.
  • The entrance is step-free and there is parking on site.
