Could an assessment help?

It is worth booking if you, or someone you care about:

  • has had a fall in the last year, even a small one
  • feels unsteady when standing up, walking or turning round
  • holds on to furniture or walls to get around at home
  • worries about falling, or goes out less because of it

Not sure? Call us on 0113 000 0000 and talk it through. We are happy to help you decide.

If someone has just fallen

If they are hurt, cannot get up, or hit their head, call 999.

For urgent medical advice that is not an emergency, call NHS 111.

What happens at your first assessment

Nothing is rushed. You can sit down whenever you need to, and a family member is welcome to come with you.

  1. You call us

    We find a day and time that suits you, and ask a few simple questions about falls and your health.

  2. Before you come

    Wear comfortable clothes and the shoes you usually wear. Please bring:

    • your glasses, if you use them
    • any walking stick or frame
    • a list of the medicines you take

  3. The appointment (60 minutes)

    We talk about any falls or wobbles, then check your strength, balance and walking with some simple tests.

  4. Afterwards

    We explain what we found in plain language and agree next steps with you. That might be exercises to do at home, a place in a class, or home visits.

Looking this up for a parent or relative?

Many of the people we see are first put in touch by a son, daughter or carer. You are very welcome to call on someone’s behalf.

It is best if they know you are calling and are happy to come. You are welcome to come to the appointment with them.

It helps if you can tell us:

  • whether they have fallen recently, and roughly when
  • how they get about now, for example with a stick or frame
  • any health conditions, and the medicines they take
  • whether the clinic or a home visit would suit them better
